Privacy Policy – Charades: Guess the Word Game

Developer: AppStackStudio (formerly Appipe) · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This privacy policy explains how AppStackStudio ("we", "us") handles information when you use Charades: Guess the Word Game ("Charades" or "the app") on Android (Google Play, package se.appipe.charades) and iOS (App Store). By using the app you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • You don't need an account. The app signs you in anonymously with a random ID.
  • The app shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Custom decks you create, your settings and your game history stay on your device (and in your device's own backup, if enabled). If you send us feedback by email, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Service providers

We use the following providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) – Firebase privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) – RevenueCat privacy
  • OpenAI (AI deck generation, only when you use that feature) – OpenAI privacy
  • Google Play and Apple App Store (purchases and billing)

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are kept for as long as needed for the purposes above, following the retention settings of the providers (Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months). Subscription records are kept as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal requirements. You can stop collection by uninstalling the app. To ask us to delete data linked to your anonymous ID, email info@appstackstudio.com; we will respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Charades is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections and the security measures of our providers to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
