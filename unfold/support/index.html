Unfold: Wallpapers – Support

Developer: AppStackStudio

Need help with Unfold? Email us at info@appstackstudio.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Quick help

  • Which image goes where? Every Unfold wallpaper comes as a pair: Outer is made for the screen you see when the phone is closed, Inner for the large screen when it's open. "Save wallpaper" saves both to Photos.
  • Setting the wallpapers. Open Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper › Photos and pick the Outer image while folded, then repeat for the Inner image while unfolded. The app's "Setting both displays" guide walks you through it.
  • Can't save to Photos. Unfold only needs permission to add photos. Go to Settings › Apps › Unfold › Photos and choose "Add Photos Only".
  • Wallpapers don't load. Check your internet connection and pull to refresh. Wallpapers you've already opened stay available offline.
  • Restoring Unfold Pro. On a new phone, open Unfold's Settings and tap "Restore purchases" (or "Restore" on the Unfold Pro screen) while signed in to the same Apple Account. Subscriptions are managed in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
